About

I'm a Mechatronics Engineer with a track record of designing and delivering complex automation systems end-to-end — from PLC logic and servo motion control to mechanical design and production commissioning.

At Startecx, I built NPI machinery for the Tesla Optimus production line. At Karkintech, I founded and ran my own engineering business — owning everything from customer RFQ intake to mold tooling, vendor qualification, and on-site machine commissioning.

I specialize in problems where no off-the-shelf solution exists — where the answer has to be engineered from first principles, built in-house, and delivered fast.
